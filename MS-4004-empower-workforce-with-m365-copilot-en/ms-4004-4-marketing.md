
# Microsoft Copilot Demo for Marketing: Exercise 1

## Demo Overview

In this scenario, a marketing professional plans marketing initiatives for a new digital service.

Use Microsoft Copilot to research the market and customer needs, analyze marketing data, and organize target customers and the direction of the initiatives.

1. Research the market and customer needs
2. Analyze marketing data
3. Develop marketing initiatives based on research and analysis
4. Create a campaign proposal

> **Demo flow: Research → Analyze → Strategize → Create**

The total demo duration is 50 minutes.

# Exercise 1-1: Research the Market and Customer Needs

## Tool

[Open Researcher](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/agents/researcher)

## Situation

You are planning marketing initiatives for a new business-to-business digital service.

First, research changes in the market and the challenges facing customer organizations.

## Prompt

```text
Research the business-to-business digital services market in Japan.

Focus on the following areas.

1. Recent major market trends
2. Common challenges facing customer organizations
3. Key considerations in purchasing decisions
4. Leading companies and services and their characteristics based on publicly available information
5. Areas where demand may grow
6. Opportunities marketers should focus on

Base your answer on publicly available information and cite your sources.

Distinguish facts from assumptions, and do not make definitive statements about information that cannot be verified.
```

## Expected Outcome

A market research report is generated covering topics such as:

- Major market trends
- Challenges facing business customers
- Key purchasing considerations
- Leading companies and services
- Market opportunities
- Marketing implications
- Sources

## Demo Delivery Tips

Researcher may take time to generate results.

You can prerecord a successful run and prepare a backup video with the waiting time removed.

# Exercise 1-2: Analyze Marketing Data

## Tool

[Open Analyst](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/agents/analyst)

## Situation

You now understand the external market.

Next, analyze data from past marketing initiatives to determine which channels and initiatives performed well.

This exercise uses demo sample data, not actual company data.

## Sample Data Example

Use a simple Excel file containing fields such as:

- Campaign
- Channel
- Impressions
- Clicks
- Leads
- Cost
- Conversions

## Prompt

```text
Analyze this marketing campaign data.

Focus on the following.

1. Performance by channel
2. Campaigns with high numbers of conversions
3. Initiatives that deliver strong results relative to cost
4. Initiatives with low performance or potential need for improvement
5. Key trends visible in the data
6. Points to consider when planning the next campaign

Organize important metrics in a format that makes comparison easy.

Do not make definitive claims about causes that cannot be verified from the data; present them as hypotheses.
```

## Expected Outcome

Analyst performs analyses such as:

- Performance comparisons by channel
- Conversion rates
- Cost efficiency
- High-performing campaigns
- Low-performing campaigns
- Trends in the data
- Points to consider for future initiatives

> This demo focuses not just on calculating numbers, but on identifying trends across multiple metrics that marketers should pay attention to.

## Follow-Up Prompt

```text
Narrow these analysis results down to the three points that marketers should pay the most attention to.

For each point, explain the following separately.

- Facts confirmed by the data
- Possible hypotheses
- What to verify next
```

# Exercise 1-3: Develop a Marketing Strategy

## App

[Open Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/home)

## Situation

Market research and marketing data analysis are complete.

Next, consider whom to target, what to communicate, and which channels to use for the new business-to-business digital service.

## Prompt

```text
We are considering marketing initiatives for a new business-to-business digital service.

Create an initial draft of marketing initiatives, assuming they will build on the market research and marketing data analysis conducted so far.

Organize the content under the following headings.

1. Primary target customers
2. Potential challenges facing the target customers
3. Key messages to communicate
4. Marketing channels to use
5. Content to create
6. Desired next customer action
7. KPIs to measure effectiveness

Do not make definitive statements about customer needs. Indicate where actual data or customer research is needed for verification.
```

## Expected Outcome

The basic elements of the marketing initiatives are organized.

For example:

- Target
- Customer Need
- Message
- Channel
- Content
- Call to Action
- KPI

The focus is not on letting Copilot make strategic decisions, but on **organizing information from market research and data analysis into actionable initiatives**.

## Follow-Up Prompt

```text
Summarize this initiative on one page so the following four points are clear.

"Whom we are targeting"
"What we are communicating"
"Which channels we are using"
"Which action we want to drive"
```

# Exercise 1-4: Create a Campaign Proposal

## App

[Open Word](https://word.cloud.microsoft/)

## Situation

The target customers and direction of the marketing initiatives have been organized.

Next, create a concrete campaign proposal for stakeholder review.

Start with a new Word document without using existing marketing materials.

## Prompt

```text
Create a marketing campaign proposal for a business-to-business digital service.

Use the following structure.

1. Campaign background
2. Campaign objectives
3. Intended target audience
4. Potential challenges facing the target audience
5. Key messages
6. Marketing channels to use
7. Content to create
8. Call to Action
9. KPIs to measure effectiveness
10. Items to confirm before launch

Keep the content concise and specific as an initial draft for the marketing team to review.

Do not make definitive statements about unverified customer needs or market information; mark them as "Requires validation."
```

## Expected Outcome

Word creates a campaign proposal covering topics such as:

- Campaign objectives
- Target audience
- Customer challenges
- Value Proposition
- Messages
- Channels
- Content
- CTA
- KPI
- Items requiring validation

## Follow-Up Prompt: Shorten for Executives

```text
Add an executive summary at the beginning of this proposal that a manager can read in about one minute.
```

## Follow-Up Prompt: Add an Execution Plan

```text
Assuming this campaign will run over eight weeks, add a high-level schedule for preparation, execution, and measurement.
```

# Exercise 1 Summary

This demo explored how a marketing professional moves from understanding the market to developing concrete initiatives.

| Stage | Marketing Activity | Tool |
|---|---|---|
| **Research** | Research the market and customer needs | [Researcher](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/agents/researcher) |
| **Analyze** | Find trends in marketing data | [Analyst](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/agents/analyst) |
| **Strategize** | Organize the target audience, messages, and channels | [Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/home) |
| **Create** | Create a campaign plan | [Word](https://word.cloud.microsoft/) |

> The key point of this exercise is not to let Copilot decide the marketing strategy itself, but to organize market facts, available data, and hypotheses so people can make their next marketing decisions more easily.

# Demo Delivery Tips

This exercise is designed to minimize dependence on actual company data or past marketing materials.

- Existing Outlook emails: Not used
- Existing Teams chats: Not used
- Past marketing materials: Not used
- Actual customer data: Not used
- Actual campaign data: Not used

Only Exercise 1-2 requires a small sample Excel file prepared in advance for analysis.

The main preparation items are therefore:

1. A sample Excel file
2. Backup results from Researcher
