
# Microsoft Copilot Demo for HR: Exercise 1

## Demo Overview

In this scenario, an HR professional reviews internal training completion and sends guidance and follow-ups to the relevant employees.

Use Microsoft Copilot to review training data, analyze trends, organize a follow-up approach, and draft notification emails.

1. Review training completion data
2. Analyze training completion status
3. Organize a follow-up approach
4. Draft notification emails for the relevant employees

> **Demo flow: Review → Analyze → Plan → Communicate**

The total demo duration is 50 minutes.

# Exercise 1-1: Review Training Completion Data

## App

[Open Excel](https://excel.cloud.microsoft/)

## Situation

Mandatory company-wide training is underway.

As the deadline approaches, review the current completion status as an HR professional and identify employees who need follow-up.

Use fictional employee data, not actual employee data.

## Sample Data

Prepare an Excel file with columns such as:

- Employee ID
- Department
- Course
- Assigned Date
- Due Date
- Completion Date
- Status

Personal names are not required.

Employee IDs may also be fictional values such as:

- E001
- E002
- E003
- E004

## Prompt

```text
Review this training completion data.

Organize the following.

1. Total number of assigned employees
2. Number who have completed training
3. Number who have not completed training
4. Completion rate
5. Employees who have not completed training and whose deadlines are approaching
6. Employees whose training is already overdue
7. Employees requiring follow-up

If needed, add columns that make training completion status easy to review.

Do not infer employee information or reasons for training participation that are not in the source data.
```

## Expected Outcome

Excel organizes information such as:

- Number of assigned employees
- Completed
- Not Completed
- Completion Rate
- Due Soon
- Overdue
- Follow-up Required

> In the HR demo, focus on organizing training completion status from the data, not on having Copilot "evaluate" employees.

## Follow-Up Prompt

```text
Make it easy to identify employees who need follow-up.

Classify them into the following three categories.

- Completed
- Due Soon
- Overdue
```

# Exercise 1-2: Analyze Training Completion Status

## App

[Open Excel](https://excel.cloud.microsoft/)

## Situation

Individual completion status has been reviewed.

Next, aggregate completion status by department to identify where follow-up is needed.

Continue using the same Excel file.

## Prompt

```text
Analyze this training completion data by department.

For each department, organize the following.

1. Number of assigned employees
2. Number who have completed training
3. Number who have not completed training
4. Completion rate
5. Number due soon
6. Number overdue

If there are differences between departments, explain them as facts confirmed by the data.

Do not speculate about causes of differences in completion rates that cannot be verified from the data.
```

## Expected Outcome

Information such as the following can be compared by department:

- Number of assigned employees
- Number who have completed training
- Number who have not completed training
- Completion rate
- Due soon
- Overdue

## Follow-Up Prompt: Create a Chart

```text
Create a chart comparing training completion status by department.

Make the differences in departmental completion rates easy to understand.
```

## Follow-Up Prompt: Organize Follow-Up Targets

```text
Organize employees to consider for follow-up in the following order.

1. Overdue
2. Due soon
3. Other incomplete training
```

> This prioritization is based on administrative deadlines, not on evaluations of individual employees' abilities or work attitudes.

# Exercise 1-3: Organize a Follow-Up Approach

## App

[Open Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat](https://copilot.cloud.microsoft/home)

## Situation

The training completion analysis has identified employees whose training is due soon or overdue.

Next, consider how to follow up with them.

## Prompt

```text
We are considering how to follow up with employees who have not completed mandatory internal training.

Organize appropriate follow-up methods for each of the following three groups.

1. Employees who have not completed training and still have plenty of time before the deadline
2. Employees who have not completed training and whose deadlines are approaching
3. Employees whose training is overdue

For each group, organize the following.

- Information to communicate
- Communication tone
- Recommended timing
- Required actions
- Conditions for considering contacting the manager

Base the approach on the facts of training completion status and deadlines. Do not speculate about reasons for non-completion or evaluate employees' attitudes or abilities.
```

## Expected Outcome

Follow-up methods are organized according to completion status.

For example:

### Plenty of Time Before the Deadline

- Standard notification
- Training purpose
- Deadline
- How to complete the training

### Due Soon

- Reminder
- Clear deadline
- Required action

### Overdue

- Notification of incomplete training
- Request for prompt action
- Check with the manager as needed

The important point is **not to have Copilot speculate about why an employee has not completed training**.

# Exercise 1-4: Draft Notification Emails

## App

[Open Outlook Mail](https://outlook.cloud.microsoft/mail/)

## Situation

The follow-up approach for employees with approaching deadlines has been established.

Next, draft a reminder email to employees.

Create a new email without using existing Outlook emails.

## Prompt

```text
Draft a reminder email to employees whose mandatory internal training deadline is approaching.

Include the following.

1. That this is a reminder about mandatory training
2. The training deadline
3. A request to complete the training by the deadline
4. A request to review how to access and complete the training
5. A request to contact the support team if there are any issues

Use concise, polite wording.

Do not speculate about reasons for non-completion or include language evaluating employees' work attitudes or abilities.

Avoid blaming language and make the required action clear.
```

## Expected Outcome

Outlook creates an email draft that can be reviewed before sending it to the relevant employees.

The email includes:

- Purpose
- Training
- Due Date
- Required Action
- Support

## Follow-Up Prompt: Make It Shorter

```text
Make the email a little shorter while preserving the required content.

Make the training deadline and required action clear at a glance.
```

## Follow-Up Prompt: Adapt for Overdue Training

```text
Adapt this email for employees whose training deadline has already passed.

Avoid blaming language and clearly state that the training is currently incomplete and what action is needed.
```

# Demo Delivery Tips

This exercise does not use actual employee information or existing internal communications.

- Actual employee information: Not used
- Actual training history: Not used
- Existing Outlook emails: Not used
- Existing Teams chats: Not used
- Actual performance evaluation data: Not used
- HR Agent: Not used
- Loop: Not used

The exercise only requires one Excel file containing fictional training completion data.

## Tips for Preparing the Sample Excel File

Fictional data for about 15-20 people is sufficient.

Intentionally include a mix of the following patterns.

- Completed
- Not completed, with plenty of time before the deadline
- A few days before the deadline
- Overdue
- Different completion rates across departments

However, it is recommended not to include "reasons" for non-completion in the data.

This lets you demonstrate the difference between:

**A verifiable fact:**
"15 of the 20 employees in Sales have completed training"

and:

**An unsupported assumption:**
"Sales employees have not completed training because they are busy."
